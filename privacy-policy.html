Privacy Policy

Learn how we protect and manage your data.

Entity: Auto Daddy

Effective Date: May 1st, 2026

Contact: info@autodaddy.ca

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Auto Daddy collects, uses, stores, and protects personal information in compliance with Canadian privacy laws, including PIPEDA. It applies to our websites, apps, APIs and related services. By using the service you agree to this policy.

2. Information We Collect

a) Personal Information

Name, Email address, Phone number, Account credentials

b) Vehicle Information

Vehicle number (plate), VIN (if provided), Service history (uploaded by user)

c) Technical Information

Device type, App usage data, IP address (for security purposes)

3. Purpose of Collection

We collect information to:

  • Operate and improve the Service
  • Connect car owners with mechanic shops
  • Secure accounts and prevent misuse
  • Communicate service-related updates

4. Data Visibility & Access

  • Vehicle data is visible only to the vehicle owner
  • Mechanic shops can view data only with customer consent
  • Auto Daddy employees access data strictly on a need-to-know basis

5. Data Sharing

We do NOT sell, rent, or trade personal information. Information may be shared only:

  • With service providers (hosting, payment processing)
  • When required by law
  • With user consent

6. Data Security

We use:

  • Encrypted data storage
  • Secure servers hosted in Canada
  • Access controls and monitoring

No system is 100% secure, but we follow industry best practices.

7. Data Retention

  • Account data is retained while the account is active
  • Users may request deletion at any time
  • Legal or compliance data may be retained as required by law

8. User Rights

Users may:

  • Access their personal data
  • Request correction or deletion
  • Withdraw consent (subject to service limitations)

Requests can be sent to: info@autodaddy.ca

9. Children’s Privacy

  • Auto Daddy does not knowingly collect data from children under 13
  • Accounts identified as belonging to minors will be deleted

10. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Continued use of the Service after changes indicates acceptance.
